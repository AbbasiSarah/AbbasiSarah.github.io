If you have ever chatted with **Claude**, you have used a product from Anthropic. Until this week, only big investors and staff could own a piece of the company. Now anyone with a brokerage account can, after one of the most-watched stock market days of the year.

## What happened

On **Thursday 19 November 2026**, Anthropic's shares started trading on the **Nasdaq** stock exchange under the ticker **ANT**. This is called an **IPO** (initial public offering): the first time a private company sells its shares to the public.

- The night before, Anthropic set the price at **$83 per share**, above its earlier target range of **$70 to $78**.
- It sold about **980 million shares** and raised about **$81.3 billion**. Forbes, Fox News and The Neuron call it the **largest IPO on record**, ahead of **SpaceX's $75 billion** IPO in June.
- The stock **opened at $111.50**, about **34% higher** than the IPO price, and reached **$115.10** at its peak.
- It then fell back and **closed at $97.17**, up about **17%** on the day.

At the close, Anthropic was worth roughly **$1.27 trillion**, according to Forbes and Yahoo Finance. (You may see other numbers, because outlets count shares in different ways.) (This isn't financial advice.)

Siblings **Dario Amodei** (CEO) and **Daniela Amodei** (president), who co-founded the company in **2021**, rang the opening bell. Dario said the company has "always been honest that this technology has very real risks."

## Why it matters

Building AI models costs huge amounts of money for chips and **data centres** (giant buildings full of computers). Selling shares is one way to pay for that. Anthropic's filings show **big spending promises** on computing power and continuing **losses**, so investors are betting that sales keep growing fast.

The wild first day also shows how **nervous** the market is. Fox News noted that the Nasdaq had dropped nearly **8%** in the past month as investors worried about an AI bubble. A stock jumping 34% and then giving back half of that gain in a few hours is a sign of that mood.

Ownership is interesting too. Forbes reports that **Amazon owns nearly 11%** and **Alphabet (Google's parent) nearly 10%**. So the big cloud companies are both partners and part-owners of the AI labs.

## What we can learn

1. **Learn the basic words.** IPO, share price, market value: these terms now appear in tech news every week. Knowing them helps you follow the AI industry.
2. **Hype and value are different.** The opening price showed excitement; the closing price showed second thoughts. Look at the facts, not the first headline.
3. **AI is expensive to build.** Behind every chatbot is a big bill for computing. That's why skills in making models smaller and cheaper are so valuable.
4. **Safety is part of the story.** Anthropic put AI risks right in its filing and its public statements. As builders, we should talk openly about risks too.
