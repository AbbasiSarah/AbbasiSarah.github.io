Just one day after Anthropic's record stock market debut, the mood on Wall Street flipped. On Friday, investors rushed to sell AI stocks, and the chipmaker behind most AI training, **Nvidia**, had one of its worst days in years.

## What happened

On **Friday 20 November 2026**, US stock markets fell hard:

- The **Nasdaq Composite** (an index full of tech companies) dropped about **5.3%** to **22,617.73**. That erased all of its gains for **2026**.
- The **S&P 500** (500 big US companies) fell roughly **3.5% to 3.7%**, depending on the source, and the **Dow** lost about **1.9%**, or nearly **950 points**.
- **Nvidia** shares fell about **14%** to **$125.53**.
- **Anthropic**, which started trading only on Thursday, fell about **8.5%** to **$88.91**, still just above its **$83** IPO price.
- An index of chip companies, the **Philadelphia Semiconductor Index**, dropped about **10.7%**.

Both the S&P 500 and the Dow entered a **correction**. Yahoo Finance defines this as a sustained drop of **10% or more** from a recent high. The S&P 500's last record close was on **6 October**. For the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq, it was the **worst week since April 2025**.

## Why it happened

There wasn't just one reason. The reporting points to a mix:

1. **Questions about Nvidia.** Investing.com and The Motley Fool point to a **Reuters report** that the US **SEC** (the regulator that polices stock markets) is looking at Nvidia over possible **accounting issues**. Earlier, on **17 November**, the Wall Street Journal reported an SEC civil probe into whether Nvidia's disclosures misled investors. This follows **Jensen Huang** stepping down as CEO on **12 November**. California's attorney general, **Rob Bonta**, has also said his office is looking into parts of the scandal. Reuters, as quoted by the Fool, reminds readers that "investigations are not evidence of wrongdoing."
2. **Fears about AI spending.** Big tech companies are borrowing heavily to build data centres. Many investors worry that AI profits won't arrive fast enough to pay for it all.
3. **Politics and interest rates.** Investing.com also mentions concerns after the US midterm elections about new rules for data centres, and worries that interest rates will stay high.

This is surprising because Nvidia's latest results were strong: revenue more than doubled to about **$115.8 billion** last quarter, according to the Fool.

## What we can learn

1. **Good results don't always mean a rising price.** Prices reflect what people *expect* next, and trust, not just last quarter's numbers.
2. **Trust and governance matter.** A leadership scandal and a regulator probe can shake even the most successful tech company.
3. **AI is still a long-term skill.** Markets go up and down, but companies, hospitals and schools are still adopting AI. Learning to build useful, efficient AI keeps its value whatever the stock market does.
4. **Read more than one source.** Even on the same day, outlets gave slightly different numbers. Checking several sources helps you see the real picture.
