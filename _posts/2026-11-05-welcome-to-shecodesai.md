Hi, I'm Sara, an AI and Data Science Specialist and trainer. **SheCodesAI** is where I share what I build and learn in artificial intelligence, in a way anyone can follow.

## What you'll find here

- **Projects:** machine learning projects I built from the ground up, each with code you can run and, where possible, a live demo you can try in your browser.
- **AI Trends:** new tools, research and ideas in AI, explained in plain words.
- **Videos:** short reels with coding tips and project walkthroughs, from my Instagram.

## Why "Code !t"

Most people who want to learn AI never start, because it looks too big. The way in is to build something small, then something slightly bigger. *Don't just dream, Code !t.*

Follow along, and tell me on Instagram what you'd like me to explain next.
